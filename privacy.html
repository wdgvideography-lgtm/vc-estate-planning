Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Who we are

VC Estate Planning Ltd (“we”) runs this website and is the “controller” of your personal information. Email: enquiries@vcestateplanning.co.uk. Phone: 07904 750856.

What we collect and why

  • Enquiries: if you use our contact form, email or call us, we receive the details you give us – such as your name, email address, phone number and what you’d like help with. We use them to reply and arrange a consultation, because you asked us to.
  • Clients: to prepare wills, lasting powers of attorney, trusts or help with probate we need personal and family details, and sometimes information about health or finances. We only collect what the work needs, and we use it to provide the service you ask for and to meet our legal and professional duties. Where we need health information we rely on your explicit consent or on it being necessary to establish or defend legal rights.
  • Website visits: our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site secure. We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies on this website.

We never sell your personal information. Please don’t include sensitive details (such as health information) in the website form – we’ll ask for what we need once we speak.

Who we share it with

  • Formspree – receives the website contact form and emails it to us.
  • Vercel – hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts – supplies the website fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Our email provider, and – only where your instructions need it – professionals such as solicitors, the Probate Registry or the Office of the Public Guardian.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, they use UK-approved safeguards.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work are deleted within 2 years. Client files are kept for as long as needed for the documents we prepare and our legal and professional obligations.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, ask us to limit or stop using it, withdraw consent, or ask for a copy. Contact enquiries@vcestateplanning.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
